Pous Toder Dak Diyechhe | Kalimba Tabs | Number Notation

DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Pous Toder Dak Diyechhe' on the kalimba with number tabs, with a light tabla (Dadra) underneath.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1923)

🎵 Tempo: 100 BPM
🎵 Scale: F Lydian
🎵 Taal: Dadra (6 matras, 3 + 3)
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): no retuning needed

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 4 (F).
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable; the quick grace notes are left out.
• The melody uses both a raised and a natural fourth. The raised one (B) is the 7 of "bhorechhe" and "shune shune"; the three natural ones (B♭), on "mo" of "mori haay", are also played on 7, a semitone high.
