Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1924)

🎵 Tempo: 88 BPM
🎵 Scale: A Minor
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): no retuning needed

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 6 (A): every "bhaalobasi" comes to rest on it.
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable; the quick grace notes are left out.
• The tune also uses a flat seventh (B♭) on a few short notes, such as the "ba" of "bhaalobasi"; they are played on 7, a semitone high.
