Pagla Hawar Badalo Dine | Kalimba Tabs | Number Notation

DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Pagla Hawar Badalo Dine' on the kalimba with number tabs, with a light tabla (Dadra) underneath.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1939)

🎵 Tempo: 110 BPM
🎵 Scale: G Mixolydian
🎵 Taal: Dadra (6 matras, 3 + 3)
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): tune 3' (E5) down to D♯5; keep E4 and E6

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 5 (G).
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable; the quick grace notes are left out.
• The retuned 3' is the flat third heard in "Gharer mukhe aar ki re" and "Ja na chaaibar"; with it every note is in tune.
