Notes on this arrangement:
• The notes follow Tagore's own notation (swaralipi). The song has no fixed taal, so there is no tabla.
• The home note is 5 (G): every "Chalo chalo, chalo bhaai" comes to rest on it.
• Every note is on the 17-key C kalimba as it is, with no notes played a semitone off.
