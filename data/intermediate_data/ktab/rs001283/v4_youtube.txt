DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Klanti Amar Khoma Karo' on the kalimba with number tabs, with a light tabla (Dadra) underneath.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1914)

🎵 Tempo: 100 BPM
🎵 Scale: A Minor
🎵 Taal: Dadra (6 matras, 3 + 3)
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): no retuning needed

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 6 (A).
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable; the quick grace notes are left out.
• The 3 notes on "ko" of "kobhu" are a B♭, played on 7 (B, a semitone high); every other note is on the kalimba as it is.
