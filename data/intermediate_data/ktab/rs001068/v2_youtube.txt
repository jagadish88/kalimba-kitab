DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Jakhon Porbe Na Mor' on the kalimba with number tabs, with a light tabla (Dadra) underneath.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1916)

🎵 Tempo: 105 BPM
🎵 Scale: C Major
🎵 Taal: Dadra (6 matras, 3 + 3)
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): no retuning needed

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 1' (C).
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable; the quick grace notes are left out.
• "Jombe dhula taanpuratar", "Emni kore baajbe bnaashi" and "ke bole go sei probhate" follow the alternative tune printed in the swaralipi, which fits the kalimba without retuning.
