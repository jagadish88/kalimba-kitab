Amar Sonar Bangla | Kalimba Tabs | Number Notation

DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Amar Sonar Bangla' on the kalimba with number tabs: the full song, all five verses.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1905)

🎵 Tempo: 72 BPM
🎵 Scale: G Mixolydian
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): tune 4' (F5) up to F♯5; keep F4

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 5 (G).
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable; the quick grace notes are left out.
• The tune uses both the natural and the flat seventh in the same octave. With 4' retuned to F♯ the natural one ("ghraane paagol kore", "mori haay") is in tune; the flat one in that octave ("ki dekhechhi", "molin hole") is played on 4', a semitone high. The low flat seventh (4, F) is in tune.
