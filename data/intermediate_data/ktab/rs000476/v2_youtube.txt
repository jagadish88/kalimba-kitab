DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Bhalobese Sokhi Nibhrite' on the kalimba with number tabs.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1901)

🎵 Tempo: 84 BPM
🎵 Scale: E Phrygian
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): no retuning needed

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 3' (E): every verse comes to rest on it.
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable; the quick grace notes are left out.
• Some verses ("Dhoriya raakhiyo", "Mone kore sokhi", "Aamar moner", "Aamar aakulo") lean on a flat sixth and flat seventh; on the kalimba as it is they are played on 6' and 7', a semitone high.
