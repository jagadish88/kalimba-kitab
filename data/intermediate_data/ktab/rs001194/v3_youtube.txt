DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Katobaro Bhebechhinu' on the kalimba with number tabs.

Written by Rabindranath Tagore (1885), to the Western tune of "Drink to Me Only with Thine Eyes".

🎵 Tempo: 170 BPM
🎵 Scale: C Major
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): no retuning needed

Notes on this arrangement:
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable.
• The last verse has 2 B♭ notes (on "shu-dhai-chho" and "pro-ka-si"); they are played on 7' (B), so that no tine has to be retuned. The highest note is 2'' (on "shu-dhai-chho").
