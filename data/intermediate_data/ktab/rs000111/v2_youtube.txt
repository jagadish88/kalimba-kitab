Alo Amar Alo Ogo | Kalimba Tabs | Number Notation

DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Alo Amar Alo Ogo' on the kalimba with number tabs, with a light tabla (Dadra) underneath.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1911)

🎵 Tempo: 90 BPM
🎵 Scale: E Phrygian
🎵 Taal: Dadra (6 matras, 3 + 3)
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): no retuning needed

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 3 (E): the refrain comes to rest on it.
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable; the quick grace notes are left out.
• The two sharp fourths on "sro" of "Aalor srote" are played on 4 (F), a semitone low.
