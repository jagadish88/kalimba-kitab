DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Mamo Chitte Niti Nritye' on the kalimba with number tabs, with a light tabla (Khemta) underneath.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1910)

🎵 Tempo: 100 BPM
🎵 Scale: G♯ Dorian
🎵 Taal: Khemta (6 matras, 3 + 3)
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): retune 5 tines
   5 (G4) → G♯4 · 6 (A4) → A♯4 · 2' (D5) → C♯5 · 3' (E5) → D♯5 · 6' (A5) → G♯5

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 5 (G♯).
• With these 5 retunes, 13 notes still fall between the tines: the 6 high F♯ notes are played on 5' (G, a semitone high) and the 7 high A♯ notes in "Ki anondo… Dibaraatri" on 6' (G♯, a tone low).
• "Taata thoi thoi" is the dancer's rhythm syllables (bols): play it crisply.
