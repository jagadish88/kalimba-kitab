Tomar Holo Shuru Amar Holo Sara | Kalimba Tabs | Number Notation

DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Tomar Holo Shuru Amar Holo Sara' on the kalimba with number tabs, with a light tabla (Teentaal) underneath.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1916)

🎵 Tempo: 100 BPM
🎵 Scale: F Lydian
🎵 Taal: Teentaal (16 matras, 4 + 4 + 4 + 4)
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): no retuning needed

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 4 (F).
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable; the quick grace notes are left out.
• Every note is on the 17-key C kalimba as it is, with no notes played a semitone off.
• The first line, "Tomar holo shuru, aamar holo saara", comes back after each part, as it is sung.
