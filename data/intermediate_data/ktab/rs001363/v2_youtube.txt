Mayabano Biharini Horini | Kalimba Tabs | Number Notation

DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Mayabano Biharini Horini' on the kalimba with number tabs, with a light tabla (Kaharwa) underneath.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1934)

🎵 Tempo: 100 BPM
🎵 Scale: F Lydian
🎵 Taal: Kaharwa (8 matras, 4 + 4)
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): no retuning needed

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 4 (F).
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable; the quick grace notes are left out.
• The 4 notes on "ri" of "horini" and "biharini" are a B♭, played on 7 (B, a semitone high); every other note is on the kalimba as it is.
