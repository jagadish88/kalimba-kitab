DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Gahano Kusumokunjo Majhe' on the kalimba with number tabs, with a light tabla (Ektaal) underneath.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1877), from Bhanusingher Padabali

🎵 Tempo: 100 BPM
🎵 Scale: G Mixolydian
🎵 Taal: Ektaal (12 matras, 3 + 3 + 3 + 3)
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): no retuning needed

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 5 (G).
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable; the quick grace notes are left out.
• Every note is on the kalimba as it is (white tines only).
