DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Majhe Majhe Tabo Dekha Pai' on the kalimba with number tabs.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1914)

🎵 Tempo: 60 BPM
🎵 Scale: D Dorian
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): no retuning needed

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 2 (D).
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable; the quick grace notes are left out.
• The tune uses both the flat and the natural third and seventh. The flat ones fit the kalimba as it is; the few natural ones (in "dekha", "aankhite", "aankhir paloke" and "chaahibo") are played on the flat tine, a semitone low.
