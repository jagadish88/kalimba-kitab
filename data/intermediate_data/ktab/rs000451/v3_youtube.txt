Baro Asha Kore | Rabindra Sangeet | Kalimba Tabs | Number Notation

DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Baro Asha Kore' (Boro asha kore esechhi go) on the kalimba with number tabs.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1882)

🎵 Tempo: 100 BPM
🎵 Scale: C Major
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): no retuning needed

Notes on this arrangement:
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable. The little turn on "ko-re" (3'-4'-3') is kept: it is the song's signature.
• Every note is on the 17-key C kalimba as it is, with no notes played a semitone off. The highest note is 2'' (on "janoni bo-le").
