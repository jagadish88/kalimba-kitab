Fagun Haway Haway | Kalimba Tabs | Number Notation

DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Fagun Haway Haway' on the kalimba with number tabs, with a light tabla (Dadra) underneath.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1915)

🎵 Tempo: 100 BPM
🎵 Scale: G Mixolydian
🎵 Taal: Dadra (6 matras, 3 + 3)
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): tune 4 (F4) up to F♯4; keep 4' (F5)

Notes on this arrangement:
• The home note is 5 (G).
• A simple version for the kalimba: the main notes of the melody, one or two on each syllable; the quick grace notes are left out.
• The low 4 (F♯) is the leading note of "Phaagun" and "Tomar"; the high 4' stays F, the flat seventh heard in "kingshuke" and "jhaauyer dole".
