DESCRIPTION
Learn to play the melody of 'Tumi Robe Nirobe' on the kalimba with number tabs.

Written & composed by Rabindranath Tagore (1895)

🎵 Tempo: 120 BPM
🎵 Scale: C Major
🎵 Tuning (17-key C kalimba): no retuning needed

Notes on this arrangement:
• The rhythm follows the Geetabitan swaralipi; the notes come from the Geetabitan MIDI.
• The few sharp 4s (F♯, 6 notes) are played on 4 (F), a semitone low, so that no tine has to be retuned.
• Simplified for the kalimba: each short syllable has one note and each long one at most two (the quick runs and turns are left out).
