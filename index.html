Practise your own tab

Type numbers 1–7. Add ' or ° for each octave up (1' = high C, 1'' = top C). Use | or a new line to start a new line. For lyrics and beat lengths, use the .ktab style: (Ja: 1/)(na: 2/) (he: 4--), where / halves a note, . adds half a beat and each - holds it one more beat. Tabs with beats in brackets, like 5(1) 3'(0.5) -(1) 0(0.5), work too.

Calibrate your kalimba

The app first listens to your room for 2 seconds, then learns each tine’s sound: its exact pitch, its overtone, the click of your pluck and how it rings into its neighbours. Pluck each highlighted tine three times, about a second apart, the way you normally play. Keep the phone or laptop where it will be when you practise.
